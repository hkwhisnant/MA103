\subheader{Exercise: Developing Objective Functions}

\noindent Maximization

I am the team lead for a National Mission Team and want to maximize the number of hours my team spends on operations. 
Formulate my objective function function and matrix vector notation. 

Now I want to maximize the rate at which I gain newly qualified Senior Cyberspace Operators for my team. 
This will allow me to increase my operational capacity in the long term. 
Qualifying as a Senior Cyberspace Operator requires both training sessions and operational experience. 
I have examined the rate at which my team members progress towards qualification, and found that I gain .04 new operators for each hour of operations I schedule, and .015 new operators for each hour of training I schedule. 
Formulate an objective function maximizing the qualification rate for my team.

\noindent Minimization

There has been a lot of complaining in my office about our new busy schedule. 
For every hour of operations I schedule, I hear 32 minutes of complaining. 
For every hour of training I schedule, I hear 14 minutes of complaining. 
Formulate an objective function minimizing the amount of complaining on my team.

Some of the complaining that I hear comes from fellow team leads (I have been hogging all of the workstation in the Joint Operations Center). 
Eager to rebuild my relationships with my peers, I want to minimize my use of the shared workstations. 
For every hour of operations that I schedule, I use 3 hours of workstation time, since my operator, analyst, and mission commander each need a workstation. 
For every hour of training that I schedule, I reduce the amount of workstation time used by 45 minutes, since my team members' friends are too busy come by and hang out. 
Formulate an objective function minimizing the amount of workstation time used by my team.

\noindent Abstract Goals

All of my analysts have been offered jobs at Microsoft. 
I am concerned about retention rates, and I want to come up with an index that reflects how weekly schedules affect my team's job satisfaction. 
Develop an objective function that maximizes job satisfaction, using operations, training sessions, and morning PT as independent variables.

Do operations, training sessions, and morning PT contribute positively or negatively to job satisfaction in your objective function? Is the relationship linear? In real life, might these variables have a non-linear relationship with job satisfaction?
