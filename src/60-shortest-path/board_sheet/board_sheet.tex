\subheader{Problem 1: Odysseus' Shortest Path}

\noindent After the fall of Troy, Odysseus must find the shortest route home to Ithaca.
From Troy (node 1), he can sail toward the land of the Lastrygonians (node 2), a journey of two years, or toward Circe's island (node 3), a journey of four years. 
Treacherous currents connect the Lastrygonians and Circe's islands to one another. 
It takes one year to get from the Lastrygonians to Circe's island, and two years to get from Circe's island to the Lastrygonians.
From the Lastrygonians, continuing on to Ithaca (node 4) takes another ten years, while from Circe's island, the journey on to Ithaca takes eight years.
